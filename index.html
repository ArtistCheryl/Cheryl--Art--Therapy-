A Canvas for Your Journey

Explore, connect, and create through the power of art.

Discover Your Story Through Art

This course is a supportive and reflective space where seniors can explore life's journey through art, honoring individual experiences and preferences. Using painting and photography, we provide a platform for you to express yourself and connect with others.

Evoke Joy

Rediscover feelings of gratitude, joy, and a renewed sense of purpose through creative expression.

Foster Connection

Connect with others and share cherished memories, fostering a precious sense of companionship and community.

Cultivate Self-Understanding

Use painting and photography as tools for personal introspection and a deeper understanding of one's life story.

Meet Cheryl A. Howell, The Artist

Course Creator
A Writer, Artist, and Poet

The vision for this course comes from a passion for combining painting and photography to help people express themselves. As a writer, artist, and poet, I believe that creative expression is a powerful tool for finding joy, connection, and a deeper sense of self-worth. My goal is to create a safe and welcoming space where seniors can explore their life's journey through art.
